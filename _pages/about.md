## Jian Shi (施简)

### 中文

我是一名后端与数据系统软件工程师，常驻杭州。我主要使用 Go、Java、Python 和 SQL，关注生产级后端服务、消息驱动系统、数据集成以及可观测性与可靠性。

我曾参与构建和运维 CMDB、资产库存与安全数据服务，将多源基础设施、云资源元数据、服务归属和安全信号整合为可行动的数据产品。我也有 Kubernetes/GitOps 平台交付、CI/CD、监控与生产问题排查经验。

我看重的不只是“让代码跑起来”，而是让系统的状态可观察、决策可解释、变更可验证。近期我正在通过实践型项目继续学习 AI agents、工作流自动化和数据驱动系统，并尝试将现有的后端/数据系统经验延伸到 AI/ML 生产系统。

我毕业于墨尔本大学，获信息技术硕士学位；本科毕业于浙江工业大学软件工程专业。

### English

I am a backend and data systems software engineer based in Hangzhou. I work primarily with Go, Java, Python, and SQL, with a focus on production backend services, message-driven systems, data integration, observability, and reliability.

My experience includes building and operating CMDB, asset inventory, and security data services that turn multi-source infrastructure, cloud metadata, service ownership, and security signals into actionable data products. I have also worked with Kubernetes/GitOps platform delivery, CI/CD, monitoring, and production troubleshooting.

I care not only about making code run, but also about making system state observable, decisions explainable, and changes verifiable. Recently, I have been extending my backend and data systems experience toward AI/ML production systems through hands-on projects in AI agents, workflow automation, and data-driven systems.

I hold a Master of Information Technology from the University of Melbourne and a Bachelor of Software Engineering from Zhejiang University of Technology.
